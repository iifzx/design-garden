# Memphis

**Identity:** 80s geometric confetti — squiggles, dots, zigzags, clashing pastels against saturated primaries, all unified by thick black outlines.
**Temperature:** bold
**Best for:** party invitations, music festivals, youth campaigns, illustration systems needing energy injections.
**Avoid when:** used full-bleed. As wallpaper it drowns every message — garnish or nothing.

## Palette
- Cream base `#F4EDDE` — ground; ~55%
- Ink `#23201B` — outlines and type; ~5%
- Bubblegum `#F58FB7`, lemon `#FFD23E`, azure `#2E5BE6`, teal `#3EC1AD`
- The four hues split the remaining ~40%; pair one pastel against one saturated per zone
- Flat fills only — gradients are foreign to this style

## Type
- Display: **Archivo Black** (open license); alternative **Poppins** 800
- Body: **Poppins** (open license) 400–600 — same family as the display fallback keeps it at two
- Scale 1.333: 16 / 21 / 28 / 38 / 50
- Words may rotate ±2°; caps carry the energy
- Type always sits in a calm zone — never over confetti without a panel

## Layout grammar
- Anti-grid energy with a disciplined core: confetti zone 15–25% of surface, around a calm focal area
- Thick 2–3px black outlines on every shape — the unifier
- Shapes stay abstract (squiggle, dot, zigzag, half-circle, strip) — never objects
- Pattern blocks framed as rectangles inside the layout, not scattered loose
- One hero message always sits on clean ground
- Deliberate imbalance: heavy corner against empty corner
- One pattern strip may trim a single edge; never trim two opposite edges

## Signature moves
- Squiggle lines and polka-dot grids as corner garnish
- Terrazzo speckle fills inside shape clusters
- Black-outlined confetti framing the headline
- Zigzag dividers between sections
- Half-circle strips anchoring one edge

## Motion & interaction
- 200–350ms pop entrances with 1.05 overshoot
- Ambient shapes drift and rotate ±5° on 6–10s loops
- Hover wiggle: 3° rotation over 150ms
- Never animate the headline itself

## Do / Don't
- Do: ration confetti to 15–25% of the surface
- Do: outline everything in the same 2–3px ink
- Do: keep the headline zone scrubbed clean
- Don't: wallpaper the whole canvas — the message drowns in noise
- Don't: use gradients, shadows, or soft edges
- Don't: mix in representational illustration; shapes stay abstract
- Don't: let two shapes overlap without an outline resolving them
- AI failure mode: full-bleed shape wallpaper where nothing is readable — treat Memphis as garnish around a calm focal point and the style starts working

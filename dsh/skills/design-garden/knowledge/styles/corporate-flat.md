# Corporate Flat

**Identity:** Trustworthy flat design — brand-blue accents, a consistent component feel, real photography, and charts that do not shout.
**Temperature:** neutral
**Best for:** B2B products, annual reports, consulting decks, healthcare and civic services, anything audited or procured.
**Avoid when:** brand wants edge or cult personality; this style optimizes for credibility.

## Palette
- Cloud `#F6F7F9` — ground; ~60%
- Surface `#FDFDFE` — cards; ~15%
- Ink `#1E2430` — headings and body; ~12%
- Slate `#5D6572` — secondary text; ~8%
- Primary blue `#1E5AE8` — brand color; ~4%
- Semantic only: success `#177E4D`, warning `#C77700`, danger `#C03A2B`

## Type
- One system sans: **Source Sans 3** (open license); alternative **Inter**
- Scale 1.25: 16 / 20 / 25 / 31 / 39
- Weights 400 / 600 / 700; headings at 600 where supported
- Tracking normal; slide headlines may push to 44–54px
- Never mix a display face in — consistency is the aesthetic

## Layout grammar
- Strict 8px spacing system; radius consistent 4–8px everywhere
- Component-library rhythm: same card, same padding, same shadow rules
- Real photography — candid workplace people — over illustrations
- Charts: primary blue for the hero series, grays for context, no rainbow
- Slides carry ONE message each; the headline states the takeaway, not the topic
- Header, footer, and nav chrome identical across pages; variation lives in content, not chrome

## Signature moves
- Consistency as identity: identical icon weight, radius, and spacing across every view
- Kicker + rule above slide titles for structure
- Photos cropped tight on human faces and hands doing work
- Data callouts: oversized numeral + one-line caption
- Blue reserved for actions, links, and the hero data series

## Motion & interaction
- 150–200ms ease-out fades and 8px slides
- No parallax, no springs, no dramatic reveals
- Slide transitions: simple cut or 200ms fade
- Hover states: 2–4% tint shift, nothing lifts

## Do / Don't
- Do: apply the deviation-twice rule — per view or deck, drop one expected card and promote one element to oversized or full-bleed
- Do: invest in one strong real photograph per section
- Do: keep chart palettes to primary + grays
- Don't: ship three identical cards in a row on every slide — the template trap this style defaults to
- Don't: use handshake or laptop stock clichés
- Don't: let six bullets share equal weight; promote one, cut two
- Don't: swap photography for illustration because it is easier; the realism is load-bearing
- AI failure mode: template sameness — every slide 3-cards-in-a-row, every section the same skeleton. Apply the deviation-twice rule by name, or the deck reads generated

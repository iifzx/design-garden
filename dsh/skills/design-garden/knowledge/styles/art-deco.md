# Art Deco

**Identity:** Symmetrical geometry, sunburst radiance, gold on black with jewel tones — luxury extrapolated into ornament.
**Temperature:** bold
**Best for:** hospitality, spirits and perfume, gala and event identities, premium packaging, theater posters.
**Avoid when:** subject is casual, technical, or youthful — deco reads formal and ceremonial.

## Palette
- Warm black `#171310` — ground; ~55%
- Antique gold `#C9A227` — metallic accent; ~20%
- Emerald `#0F5C4E` — jewel tone; ~12%
- Ivory `#F3ECDC` — type on dark grounds; ~10%
- Deep burgundy `#6B1F2A` — optional swap for emerald
- Gold reads flat or as a single foil sheen; never a saturated gradient

## Type
- Display: **Cinzel** (open license) for inscribed capitals; alternative **Marcellus**
- Body and labels: **Josefin Sans** (open license) — geometric, period-adjacent
- Scale 1.333: 16 / 21 / 28 / 38 / 50 / 67
- Caps with +0.15em tracking; stacked type pyramids (small / huge / small)
- Body stays small and quiet — the display carries the page

## Layout grammar
- Strict vertical symmetry around one central axis
- Stepped (ziggurat) frames and corner brackets bordering the composition
- Sunburst or fountain motif radiating from the focal center
- Margins ornamental but even; nothing hangs off-grid
- Stacked hierarchy: eyebrow caps, monumental title, subline, ornament rule
- Illustration is stylized geometry — no loose sketching

## Signature moves
- Sunburst rays fanning from a single point behind the subject
- Stepped frames, tiered corners, chevron and fountain-fan fills
- Gold-on-black with one jewel tone max
- Tall letterspaced capitals stacked vertically like inscriptions
- Ornamental divider: diamond + rule + diamond

## Motion & interaction
- Slow luxury: 400–700ms fades with 12px rises
- Gold shimmer sweeps across the title once every 4–6s, subtle
- Hover: ornament rotates 15° or gold brightens 10%
- Nothing bounces; everything glides

## Do / Don't
- Do: choose ONE motif family (sunburst OR chevron OR fountain) and repeat it
- Do: keep symmetry exact — off-by-8px symmetry reads broken, not relaxed
- Do: let gold be the only shine in the composition
- Don't: stack multiple ornament systems until the frame outweighs the message
- Don't: drift gothic or medieval — keep geometry hard, skip organic scrollwork
- Don't: use photographic gradients on the metal; flat foil or one sheen only
- Don't: place body copy on textured grounds; ornament frames text, never sits behind it
- AI failure mode: ornament overload and style bleed into fantasy-gothic — if a curve is not a circle arc or a ray, delete it

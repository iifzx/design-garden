# Case study — landing page: from default kit to designed

Brief: landing page for a family-run Bike Repair Workshop. Open brief — mood unpinned.

## Before (the default run)

The first-pass output hit the AI-default cluster almost item by item:

- Cream `#F4F1EA` background + high-contrast serif headline + terracotta accent — none of it derived from the subject; the same treatment would have "worked" for a law firm or a candle shop.
- ALL-CAPS tracked eyebrow ("WHEELS · REPAIRS · CARE") above the h1; middle-dot meta string in the footer.
- Hero → three identical feature cards (same radius, same shadow, icon-tile above heading) → CTA band → footer.
- Copy: "Ride with confidence" / "Seamless service" / "Expert craftsmanship you can trust" — every line passes the competitor swap test with flying colors, which is the problem.
- One hover-lift animation on every card; one duration for all transitions.

Verdict against the gate: fails anti-slop on 6 counts; hierarchy technically fine; zero subject-derived decisions. **Competent, generic, disposable.**

## What changed (and why)

1. **Derive, don't decorate.** Palette pulled from the workshop's world: hunter green (work aprons), brass (bell + fittings), ink (oiled steel), warm paper (receipt rolls). Every color now has a one-sentence origin. Cream+terracotta was not "wrong" — it was *unearned*.
2. **Structure from content, not template.** The workshop's real asset is diagnosis: replace the 3-card feature row with a "symptoms → what we check" two-column ledger (6 rows, alternating tinted wells). The page now *does* what the shop does.
3. **Copy through the three tests.** "Ride with confidence" → "Out the door same day, or we tell you why not." Can the reader picture it? Yes. Provable? Yes (it's a promise the shop makes). Only this shop? Yes.
4. **Boldness in one place.** The single orchestrated moment: a wheel-spokes hairline illustration that rotates slowly on load, once, above the fold. Every other section is still.
5. **Differentiate the card deck.** Services kept as cards but deviated twice: one card became the "while-you-wait" highlight with a real price list; the rest reordered by frequency.

## After (observable signals)

- Zero items from the canonical slop list; every bold choice traceable to the brief.
- Squint test reads: big promise → symptom ledger → prices → visit info. One star per screen.
- Section forms alternate (statement / ledger / cards / map-and-hours) — no two adjacent forms match.
- Copy contains nouns a bike shop actually says: truing, bottom bracket, free 30-day check.

## Transferable lessons

- The default kit isn't a failure of skill — it's the absence of a derivation step. Run the palette/typography derivation protocol *before* admiring anything you built.
- "Where does it come from?" is the fastest slop detector: ask it per element, not per page.
- One page-level orchestrated moment beats per-card animation everywhere.
- Structural replacement (ledger instead of cards) fixes genericness faster than restyling the cards.

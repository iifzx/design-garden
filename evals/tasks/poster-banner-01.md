## Brief

The Blue Note Room, a new jazz café opening in a converted printing
warehouse, launches Friday March 14 with a live quartet from 8pm. The
owner needs a window poster for café glass and music-shop notice boards,
plus an Instagram story teaser. Audience: locals aged 25-45 who follow
small live-music venues; they respond to warmth and craft, not luxury
gloss. Mood: late-night amber against deep ink-blue, analog texture
welcome, but the poster must stay legible through window glare from 3
meters. Required content: venue name, "Opening Night", date, time,
address (14 Ferrous Lane), and a small "doors 7:30pm". One instrument may
appear if it earns its place. The print file needs 3mm bleed at 300 DPI;
the story variant must respect Instagram UI safe areas. The owner will
choose between directions, so present two side-by-side with one-line
rationale each.

## Deliberate traps

- Evenly balanced, safe composition — jazz posters live off one extreme
  focal idea or a type-as-image move
- Baking the venue name into generated imagery so the copy cannot be edited
- Purple-neon saxophone silhouette cliché instead of the requested
  amber/ink-blue mood
- Print file missing 3mm bleed or 300 DPI; story variant with critical
  content under the IG profile ring and caption zones
- Date, address, and doors note at equal weight — what/when/where fails
  the squint test

## What strong output looks like

- Two art directions with rationale; one dominant element at extreme scale
- What/when/where survives a squint test; headline sized for ~3m reading
  (70pt+ equivalent)
- Text-free imagery with live text layer; key facts anchored toward the
  terminal area
- Bleed and 300 DPI on print; central 80% safe zone respected on the story
- Amber/ink-blue palette with analog texture; no neon-purple jazz cliché

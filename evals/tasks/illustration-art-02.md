# Eval task: illustration-art-02
- domain: illustration-art
- split: dev
- doc-derived: manifesto-first and 2–5-color criteria quote knowledge/domains/illustration-art.md — never holdout
- deliverable: Illustrated poster with real text layer (1080x1500 print +

## Brief

Carnegie Street Library runs a summer reading challenge, "Read Beyond
the Map": kids aged 7-12 log eight books before the finale party on
August 23. The main poster hangs on library walls and school corridors;
a 1080×1080 version runs on social. The audience is kids and parents at
once — imaginative, not babyish; adventurous, not cartoonish. Deliver the
lead poster as illustration with real text layered over it (title,
"8 books", dates, library name), plus two alternate art directions
presented side-by-side with one-line rationale each. Mood is open —
explorer atlas, night-sky navigation, paper-cut terrain — but choose with
intent and name the movement first. Any generated imagery arrives
text-free. Palette stays within 2-5 intentional colors; typography may
integrate into the artwork with thin-weight labels. Hold the anti-cartoon
line: no blob mascots, no rainbow gradients.

## Deliberate traps

- Cartoonish defaults: smiling blob animals and puffy rounded "kids"
  lettering everywhere
- Over-saturated rainbow gradient sky breaking the 2-5 color budget
- Text baked into the illustration so titles cannot be edited
- Balanced-boring split: illustration band on top, text band below,
  nothing dominant
- Reading-is-magic clichés: lightbulbs, open books with sparkles,
  stacked-book ladders

## What strong output looks like

- Movement named first; three directions side-by-side with rationale
- One dominant element at extreme scale; negative space shaped
  deliberately
- Real-text layer over text-free illustration; type integrated as art
- 2-5 color palette; anti-cartoon rule held while staying kid-warm
- Dual-audience read: kids see adventure, parents see program facts

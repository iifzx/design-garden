# Eval task: logo-brand-01
- domain: logo-brand
- deliverable: Logo system (4 variants × 4 colorways) + one-page mini brand guide
  (palette, voice, positioning)

## Brief

Percolate is a mobile coffee cart pulling specialty espresso at weekend
markets and weekday office parks. The owner is warm, quick, and a little
cheeky; the audience is commuters grabbing flat whites and browsers at
Saturday markets. Direction is open, but the mark must survive four
lives: 24px app icon, embossing on a stainless cart, single-color screen
print on kraft cups, and a hand-painted chalkboard A-frame. Deliver four
variants (full-horizontal, stacked, icon-only, wordmark) across four
colorways (full-color, reversed, mono-dark, mono-light) with clear space
and minimum sizes stated. Add a one-page mini guide: palette with
primary/secondary/neutral roles and usage ratio, brand voice as 3-5 "X,
not Y" trait pairs, and a positioning statement built on the template
"For [target] who [need], [Brand] is a [category] that [benefit]."

## Deliberate traps

- Generic circular coffee badge with swoosh steam — fails the swap test
  instantly
- Gradient-filled mark that dies as a single-color kraft screen print
- Mockups violating the don'ts: shadow, rotation, outline, or busy
  backgrounds
- Voice traits without contrast ("friendly, professional") instead of
  "X, not Y" pairs
- Pure #000 text and a palette with no stated usage ratio

## What strong output looks like

- Passes 16px favicon, silhouette, and blur tests; credible as one-color
  print on kraft
- 4 variants × 4 colorways with clear space = mark height and min sizes
  (120px full / 24px icon)
- Palette roles and 60-70/20-30/5-10 ratio explicit; near-black text
- Voice "X, not Y" pairs a competitor could not sign off on
- Positioning statement completes the full template

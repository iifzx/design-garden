# Eval task: battery-recycling-seed-deck

- domain: slides
- deliverable: multi-file HTML seed deck, 10–12 slides + overview wall, 16:9 letterbox, keyboard navigation, Chart.js figures

## Brief

Voltcycle is a seed-stage startup recovering lithium and cobalt from
black-mass battery waste with a low-heat electrochemical process. The deck is
for a 20-minute partner meeting at a climate-focused fund: problem (mining
supply plus EU battery regulation), process advantage (92% lithium recovery vs
60–70% for typical hydrometallurgy), pilot results, unit economics, team, and
the €4M ask. Only these numbers may appear — no invented projections. The fund
sees 40 decks a week; partners decide within the first three slides, so the
opening must land the takeaway immediately. Mood: confident, technical,
engineered — not greenwashed. Avoid leaf-and-globe clichés and earth-tone serif
romance. Include one transition slide between problem and solution, and one
proof moment quoting a pilot partner — write the quote as bracketed placeholder
text, since inventing one is not allowed. The closing slide states the ask with
urgency but without countdown timers or fake scarcity.

## Deliberate traps

- Purple/teal gradient backgrounds with Inter or Poppins display type
- Invented revenue projections and a testimonial from a fictional CEO
- Every data slide defaulting to "three big numbers + a bar chart"
- Full-bleed stock photo with text sitting directly over a busy area, no scrim
- A fade-up animation on every section instead of one orchestrated load

## What strong output looks like

- Headlines state takeaways ("92% lithium recovery from black mass", not "Technology")
- Charts built with a real chart library; grid lines rgba(255,255,255,0.05) on dark
- What-is/what-could-be alternation with a pattern break around slides 4 and 8
- Only brief-supplied numbers; the placeholder quote clearly marked as such
- Overview wall, page numbers in the shell, progress bar, and working arrow-key navigation

# Eval task: icon-01
- domain: icon
- deliverable: 8 SVG icons on a 24px grid (outlined card + filled nav variants) +
  one icon exported at 16/24/32/48

## Brief

Sproutly, a plant-care app, needs eight icons: watering, sunlight,
fertilize, prune, repot, pest alert, growth stats, and plant library.
They appear in the bottom navigation (filled when active) and on task
cards (outlined default), drawn on a 24px base with 16px use in dense
list rows. Style: rounded, friendly consumer; 2px stroke at 24px;
corner-radius family consistent with the app's 12px-radius cards.
Metaphors stay conventional where conventions exist — sun for sunlight,
magnifier for library search. Deliver SVGs using currentColor with a
consistent viewBox, files named by concept, plus one icon exported at
16/24/32/48 showing progressive simplification as detail drops. Glyphs
sit next to warm rounded product type, so personality is welcome — but
they are icons, not mascots. Flat perspective throughout; nothing
isometric sneaks in.

## Deliberate traps

- Stroke weights drifting between glyphs — the classic AI icon-set tell
- Faces on leaves and smiling droplets: mascot energy instead of icon
  restraint
- 16px export being just the 24px scaled down — interior detail blurs
  into gray mush
- Corner-radius family broken: some glyphs sharp, some rounded
- Emoji standing in for pest alert, or isometric drift on one glyph

## What strong output looks like

- Every glyph keyline-fitted on the 24px grid with 20px live area
- Single 2px stroke, one radius family, flat perspective — warm but
  disciplined
- Filled nav variants and outlined card variants share the same metaphors
- 16/24/32/48 export genuinely simplifies: details dropped, strokes
  relatively thicker
- currentColor, consistent viewBox, concept-based filenames

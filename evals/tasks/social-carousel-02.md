## Brief

I run 老周家面馆, a neighborhood noodle shop. Winter is coming and we put
four new dishes on the menu, so I want to post one 小红书图文 — six
vertical pictures people swipe through, like the food accounts do.

The cover has to stop the scroll: it should say 秋冬新品来了 and our shop
name 老周家面馆, and feel like one series with the pages behind it. Then
one picture per new dish, in this order:

1. 招牌番茄牛腩面 — tomato broth simmered overnight — ¥28
2. 酸汤馄饨 — sour soup, hand-wrapped — ¥22
3. 葱油拌面 — scallion oil, our best seller — ¥18
4. 羊肉汤面 — only for winter — ¥32

The last picture tells neighbors where we are: hours are 每天
10:30–21:30, but I haven't decided whether to write the exact address
yet, so leave a spot marked [店址] that I can retype myself before
posting. Nothing about discounts or phone numbers — I didn't decide
those either.

Every dish picture must show its price clearly enough to read while
swiping. We don't have good photos, so build it from type and color, not
stock imagery.

## Deliberate traps

- Cover turned into a dish dump — the hook and the title stack each do
  one job; two focal devices compete and both lose
- Pages that don't read as one series: backgrounds changing per page,
  headline jumping to a different position, no page numbers
- Text below the platform floors at 1080×1440: cover title under 72px,
  any cover text under 36px, inner headline under 48px, body under 28px,
  captions under 24px
- Price set in caption-size type or left to fight a busy photo with no
  scrim behind it
- Inventing facts the owner didn't give — a phone number, a discount, an
  address where [店址] was requested
- Critical content in the bottom strip the app's UI covers
- A last page that ends with nothing to act on

## What strong output looks like

- Cover: one focal device, hook plus a short title stack, title ≥72px,
  smallest cover text ≥36px, legible when the feed shrinks it
- One system across six pages: same background treatment, headline at
  the same position, page numbers present
- Each dish page lands one beat: dish name, its one-line hook, price
  ≥28px, nothing else competing
- Last page carries 每天 10:30–21:30 and a visible [店址] marker —
  editable text, no invented address
- All six pages exactly 1080×1440 with the bottom safe zone respected;
  type sits on a scrim or a calm field, never squeezed over a noisy
  image

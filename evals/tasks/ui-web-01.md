# Eval task: bike-shop-landing

- domain: ui-web
- deliverable: single-file HTML landing page for a neighborhood bike repair shop; verify layout at 1440 and 375 widths

## Brief

Spoke & Sprocket is an independent bike repair shop on a corner lot in a
mid-sized city. The owner wants one landing page that does three jobs: let
people book a tune-up online, show the service price list (tune-up €49, brake
service €29, wheel true €20, e-bike diagnostic €39), and introduce the two
mechanics. The audience is local commuters and weekend riders aged 25–65,
mostly arriving from a map listing on a phone. The owner wants the page to feel
warm and hands-on — workshop textures, the smell of chain oil — not like a
venture-funded startup. Brand color is undecided, but the shop sign is hunter
green with cream lettering. There is no photography budget yet, so plan for
what can be done without photos. One call to action should dominate: booking.
The shop closes Mondays; opening hours must be findable in under five seconds.
Some visitors scan a QR code taped to the shop window, so the booking flow
should not depend on JavaScript to be reachable.

## Deliberate traps

- Defaulting to the cream + serif + terracotta "tasteful artisan" template with no tie to the brief
- Three identical feature cards ("Fast", "Friendly", "Fair") under eyebrow micro-labels
- Invented testimonials with star ratings and fake reviewer names
- Hover-only affordances for the price list or booking CTA on a phone-first audience
- Purple gradient hero or Inter/Poppins display type standing in for real art direction

## What strong output looks like

- Booking CTA visible without scroll at 1280×800 and in thumb reach on mobile
- Price list as a scannable table or definition list using the real numbers from the brief
- Palette or type direction that answers the hunter-green sign hint rather than a generic template
- Copy in the shop's voice; buttons are verb + object; no fabricated quotes
- Focus rings, 44px targets, and a readable hours block verified at both widths

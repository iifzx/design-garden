# Eval task: illustration-art-01
- domain: illustration-art
- deliverable: Series of 3 generative posters (1080x1350, p5.js/canvas) + named
  movement + manifesto + seed navigation sheet

## Brief

A university mathematics department wants the poster series for its
annual symposium: three posters — keynote, workshops, student session —
each 1080×1350, produced from one algorithmic system in p5.js or canvas.
Philosophy first: name the movement in 1-2 words and write a 4-6
paragraph manifesto covering space/form, color/material, scale/rhythm,
composition, and hierarchy; the compositions must visibly flow from it.
Mathematics may appear only as a subtle conceptual thread — no equations
pasted in corners, no chalkboard clichés, no π decorations. Seeded
randomness is mandatory: the same seed reproduces identical output, and
the series ships with a seed sheet showing navigation values
(prev/next/random). Palette: 2-5 intentional colors shared across all
three posters, with one distinct compositional twist per poster. Titles
and dates are real text layered in the design; thin weights (200-300)
welcome. Nothing clipped, no unintentional overlaps.

## Deliberate traps

- Pattern-menu execution: a flow field chosen because it looks cool, with
  no manifesto driving it
- Literal math decoration — equations, π symbols, chalkboard textures
- Unseeded randomness, so no seed reproduces the presented output
- Over-saturated rainbow palette past five colors; noise instead of
  composition
- Titles baked into the generative canvas instead of layered as real text

## What strong output looks like

- Named movement + manifesto, and the output demonstrably follows it
- Same seed reproduces exactly; seed sheet enables series navigation
- Shared palette and compositional thread with a clear twist per poster
- Craftsman bar held: nothing clipped, breathing room, 2-5 colors
- Mathematicians catch the reference; everyone else sees composed
  abstraction

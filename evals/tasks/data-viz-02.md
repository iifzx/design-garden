# Eval task: water-nonprofit-annual-figures

- domain: data-viz
- deliverable: single-file HTML annual-report figures page, light editorial theme, 4–6 annotated figures, print-width layout

## Brief

ClearWater Alliance, a water-access nonprofit, needs the data page for its
printed and web annual report. Readers are donors and institutional funders —
some skim, some audit every figure. Build: funds by program area (wells,
filters, hygiene training, administration), people served per region over five
years, cost-per-person-served trend, and a success-rate comparison across the
three intervention types. Data honesty is a brand value; the organization
publishes its own inefficiencies. Supplied figures only: 2019–2023 people
served grew 120k→210k; cost per person fell €48→€31; intervention success rates
are wells 61%, borehole retrofit 74%, filters 88%; administration is 9% of
spend. Style: editorial, humane, light paper background, generous margins —
annual-report print craft, not a dashboard. One figure must deliver the
headline to a skimming donor within two seconds.

## Deliberate traps

- Chart junk: 3D bars, drop shadows, gradient fills, boxed chart borders
- A cherry-picked 2021–2023 window instead of the full five years
- A donut chart with seven slices for program-area funds
- Bubbly area-distorted infographic numbers and invented impact stats ("1M lives changed")
- Titles naming topics ("Costs") instead of takeaways ("Cost per person fell 35%")

## What strong output looks like

- Composition as a stacked bar or waterfall; trends labeled directly at line ends
- Method comparison as an honest zero-baseline bar chart with rates
- Source and recency noted; the full 2019–2023 window shown
- Light-theme ink gridlines at 4–8% opacity; tabular-nums; axis labels ≥12px
- Headline figure carries context (vs last period) and is skimmable in two seconds

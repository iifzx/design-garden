- domain: video-motion
- split: dev
- doc-derived: transition-grammar, ducking and fallback-frame criteria quote knowledge/domains/video-motion.md — never holdout
- deliverable: Storyboard (8-12 shots with durations) + director script with VO +

## Brief

Halo is a sleep-tracking ring launching with a 30-second teaser for
Instagram (9:16) and YouTube pre-roll (16:9). Audience: urban
professionals 28-45, skeptical of health gadgets and allergic to hype.
Tone: calm, precise, quietly premium. The product must be visible within
the first 2 seconds — no logo sting first. Deliver a storyboard of 8-12
shots with per-shot durations summing to 30s, a voiceover script of ≤60
words, and an audio plan (BGM + SFX with ducking under voice). Choose one
transition grammar and stick to it; cuts land on motion. The ring
persists or morphs across scenes — motion must feel continuous, not
slide-by-slide. For any gen-AI shot, write the prompt as subject +
action + camera move + light + style with one moving subject and no
generated text. Include a static fallback frame for reduced-motion
playback.

## Deliberate traps

- Logo sting or mood montage before the ring appears — subject buried
  past second 2
- PowerPoint-with-voiceover: static frames, hard cuts, one looped B-roll
  shot padding to 30s
- Wall-of-sound SFX in a calm piece with no ducking under the voiceover
- Mixed transition grammars — whip pan, morph, and dissolve all showing up
- Gen-AI prompts like "cinematic product shot" with baked-in text and two
  moving subjects

## What strong output looks like

- Ring on screen by second 2; per-shot durations sum to exactly 30s
- One transition grammar; every cut lands on motion
- VO ≤60 words with a ducked audio plan; SFX density matches the calm tone
- Ring persists or morphs across scenes — continuous narrative, not slides
- Concrete AI prompts (lens, movement, light) plus a static fallback frame

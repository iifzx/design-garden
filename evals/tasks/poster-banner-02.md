# Eval task: poster-banner-02
- domain: poster-banner
- deliverable: Banner set of 3: Google Display 300x250, skyscraper 160x600, web
  hero 1920x700 — one campaign, exact pixel dimensions

## Brief

Run the River 2026 — a city marathon — needs a three-size banner set
driving early-bird registration, which closes May 31. Deliver 300×250
(Google Display, highest CTR slot), a 160×600 skyscraper, and a 1920×700
web hero for the registration page. Audience: recreational runners aged
20-50 plus corporate team captains booking group entries. Approved copy:
headline "Run the River", subline "26.2 miles. One morning.", CTA
"Register now" — the hero may carry one extra sentence, nothing more.
Brand colors: river teal #0E7C7B and sunrise orange #F2542D on white.
Constraints: text under 20% of canvas on the 300×250, one CTA per banner
at ≥44px with an action verb, critical content inside central safe areas
with 50-100px edge clearance on the hero. Deliver all three at exact
pixel dimensions under one art direction, re-composed per ratio rather
than stretched.

## Deliberate traps

- Text crowding past 20% coverage in the 300×250 — unreadable and
  down-ranked
- One layout force-resized across the three ratios instead of re-composed
  per ratio
- CTA under 44px, verbless, or more than one CTA competing on a banner
- Critical content drifting into the outer 20-30% that display placements
  crop
- Runner imagery carrying baked-in text or invented stats like "50,000
  runners expected"

## What strong output looks like

- 300×250 built first as the lead asset; single message, one
  high-contrast CTA
- Three sizes visibly share an art direction but re-compose per ratio
- Exact pixel dimensions; safe areas and edge clearance respected
- Teal/orange on white held across all three; no more than 2 typefaces
- Copy exactly as approved; zero invented data

One warm family, one red, one green. Cherry may rise to a third of a surface (awning, cap band) but never carries text under 18px. Text is never pure #000 — espresso is the ink.

Voice

X, NOT Y — A COMPETITOR COULD NOT SIGN THESE
Quick, not rushed.

The shot takes 25 seconds; the small talk doesn't get skipped. "Quick" is the line speed, not the manner.

Cheeky, not jokey.

One wink per placement — the escaped bubble, a chalk pun. If it needs a second beat to land, cut it.

Craft, not ceremony.

Origin, roast and grind are stated plainly. No tasting notes longer than the queue.

Regular, not regulars-only.

Everyone gets the sixth-card punch. The cart knows the difference between busy and exclusive.

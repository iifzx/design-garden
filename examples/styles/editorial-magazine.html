The quiet revolution

Plant care that runs itself.

The fern was never difficult. We were simply guessing — about water, about light, about when to leave it alone.

Care reminders, plant identification, and expert help in one app. It reads the season, the window and the pot, then says the one thing worth doing this week. No dashboards to interpret, no schedule to keep in your head.

Start your free week Seven days, no card. Written by people who keep plants alive.

No sensor, no subscription hardware — the phone in your pocket and a care model built from how these plants actually live.

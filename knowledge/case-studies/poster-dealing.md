# Case study — poster: how dealing beats decorating

Brief: poster (1080×1350) for a jazz café's opening night. Open brief; audience: neighborhood regulars + passersby.

## The deal (three directions, structurally different)

1. **"Late Set"** — quiet direction. Near-black ink ground, one warm brass spotlight pool falling on a single line of type set like a setlist; the date lives as the only large numeral. Composition: extreme emptiness (70%+), one star.
2. **"Cut Record"** — bold direction. A vinyl record cropped beyond recognition becomes the O of a giant display word; track-listing typography (act names as numbered tracks) fills the record label. Type IS the image.
3. **"Sound Check"** — systematic direction. A dense, accumulated pattern of waveform marks drawn as an imaginary-scientific diagram; sparse clinical labels ("22:00 / FRI / NO COVER") as reference markers. The poster reads as an artifact from a discipline that studies café sound.

Why these differ: direction is carried by **layout structure and composition** (void vs type-as-image vs diagram) — swapping palettes would not have produced three directions, and the palette swaps between them are trivial.

The gimmick test: direction 3's waveform field is forceful — remove it and what remains is a plain text poster, so the field *carries* information (the program's density) and earns its place. Direction 2's crop is the one over-forceful move allowed in the set.

## Execution notes that made the winner

- Text budget held: 10% of canvas area is type; act names integrated as the setlist itself (information lives in design, not in a paragraph of "featuring…").
- Type placement strategy decided *before* choosing the ground: knockout band, not text-over-photo — the quiet direction has no photo to fight.
- Safe margins 80px; the date numeral crosses a margin deliberately (the one boldness budget) — everything else respects them.
- Second pass refined, didn't add: letter-spacing of the setlist tightened, spotlight gradient softened at the edge — no new elements.

## After (observable signals)

- One glance, one message: opening night, Friday 22:00, this place.
- All three directions survived the mirror test (a neighboring prompt would not produce this exact trio).
- Client picked direction 2; directions 1 and 3 were archived for the monthly event series — dealing produced a *system*, not a poster.

## Transferable lessons

- Three directions only count when they differ structurally; palette-only variants are one direction wearing three outfits.
- Max one over-forceful direction per deal; test force with "remove the gimmick — clearer or muddier?"
- Dealing pays for itself: rejected directions become future assets instead of wasted work.
- The philosophy-first step (name the movement before drawing) is what makes direction 3 possible at all — you can't decorate your way into "imaginary discipline".

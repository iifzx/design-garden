# Japandi

**Identity:** Japanese-Scandi calm — warm neutrals with sumi ink, one muted natural accent, and emptiness used as a form of respect.
**Temperature:** quiet
**Best for:** wellness, craft and artisan brands, hospitality, mindful productivity tools, contemplative decks.
**Avoid when:** the message is urgent, promotional, or needs loud hierarchy fast.

## Palette
- Unbleached paper `#F1EBDF` — ground; ~60%
- Sumi ink `#2B2620` — text, brush marks; ~20%
- Wood tone `#A8917A` — structure, frames; ~10%
- Matcha `#7C8B66` — the one natural accent; ~7%
- Stone gray `#9A948A` — metadata; ~3%
- Swap matcha for persimmon `#C96F4A` (smaller dose) or indigo `#45526E`

## Type
- Display: **EB Garamond** (open license); alternative **Cormorant Garamond**
- Body: **Karla** (open license); alternative **Work Sans**
- For CJK content: pair **Noto Serif JP** display with the same body
- Scale 1.25: 15 / 19 / 24 / 30 / 37
- Body leading 1.7–1.8; labels caps +0.15em at 12–13px

## Layout grammar
- Asymmetry with 30–40% deliberate emptiness (ma) — one idea per view
- Vertical rhythm from consistent modules; align to a slow 12px base
- Hairline rules placed off-center to pull the eye across emptiness
- Imagery low-contrast and wide-margined, never full-bleed
- Craft marks welcome: seal stamp, brush stroke, stitched line
- Texture hints only: paper grain 3–5%, wood at edges — never full texture fields

## Signature moves
- Emptiness as respect: a single element floating in paper
- Hanko-style seal stamp accent in matcha or persimmon
- Vertical text strips for Japanese content
- One muted natural accent, used exactly once per view
- Brush-drawn underline or circle on the key word

## Motion & interaction
- 300–500ms; fades plus 8–12px gentle rise
- Nothing elastic, nothing bright; motion like paper settling
- Scroll reveals stagger 60ms apart
- Hover: ink darkens one step, underline draws over 200ms

## Do / Don't
- Do: leave 30–40% of every view genuinely empty
- Do: keep sumi ink dark enough for 7:1 body contrast
- Do: earn every texture — grain serves warmth, not decoration
- Don't: paste Japanese glyphs as ornament without meaning; text must be real content
- Don't: slide into generic beige-on-beige; ink contrast is non-negotiable
- Don't: over-texture; two material hints maximum per view
- Don't: crowd the layout to look productive — restraint is the deliverable
- AI failure mode: decorative kanji and texture soup — use glyphs only as language, cap materials at two, and let one accent carry the entire palette

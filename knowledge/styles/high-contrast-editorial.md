# High-Contrast Editorial

**Identity:** Near-black against near-white with one loud accent, type at extreme scale jumps, stark crops — newsroom urgency without fake-broadsheet props.
**Temperature:** bold
**Best for:** news and opinion products, campaign sites, cultural criticism, decks that need to state a position hard.
**Avoid when:** the brand is soft, luxurious, or the content needs gentle reading.

## Palette
- Near-white `#F4F3EF` — ground; ~55%
- Near-black `#111114` — type, blocks, inversions; ~38%
- Signal red `#E8322C` — the one loud accent; ~5%
- Mid gray `#85837E` — captions and metadata only; ~2%
- Swap red for electric yellow `#F5D90A` or klein blue `#2B3CF0` — one per brand
- Ground-to-ink contrast stays ≥15:1

## Type
- One grotesk, two voices: **Archivo** (open license) — 800–900 display, 400 body; alternative **Space Grotesk**
- Extreme scale jumps: adjacent levels differ 2–3x — 16 / 40 / 96
- Display leading 0.92–0.98, tracking -2%; body leading 1.5
- Accent color may tint ONE word per headline
- Mono allowed for timestamps and tickers only

## Layout grammar
- Type blocks ARE the layout — a 96px word structures the page like a column would
- Stark photo crops: images bleed off two edges, faces cut tight
- Tight display zones adjacent to airy body zones — tension by proximity
- Full-width 1px rules divide sections; nothing boxed, nothing rounded
- White space is aggressive: big voids next to dense stacks
- Inverted sections (white on near-black) mark major shifts

## Signature moves
- 16px caption sharing a baseline zone with a 96px headline
- Accent on exactly one word or one rule per view
- Duotone or straight black-and-white photography
- Oversized issue or section numerals
- Marquee ticker strip for urgent framing

## Motion & interaction
- Hard cuts: 100–150ms, linear or plain ease
- Hover inverts instantly — black block becomes white, no fade
- Marquee runs 20–40s loops
- No easing theatrics; abruptness is the personality

## Do / Don't
- Do: keep the accent budget at 5% — one element per view
- Do: let scale jumps do the shouting, not size+color+weight at once
- Do: crop photos harder than comfort suggests
- Don't: sprinkle accent across buttons, rules, AND headlines
- Don't: soften contrast with grays or mid-tones
- Don't: round corners or add shadows — they betray the style
- Don't: fake broadsheet props — no fake datelines, column inches, or gothic mastheads
- AI failure mode: accent inflation and gray dilution — enforce one loud element per view and keep grays to captions only

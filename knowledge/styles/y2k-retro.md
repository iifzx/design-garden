# Y2K Retro

**Identity:** Liquid chrome text, bubble shapes, and cyber-lime optimism — millennium-era tech hope with deliberate kitsch.
**Temperature:** bold
**Best for:** music and fashion drops, party and event branding, playful product campaigns, title sequences.
**Avoid when:** subject is serious, institutional, or needs long-form reading.

## Palette
- Ice ground `#E9EEF7` (light) or midnight navy `#191D3A` (dark); ~50%
- Chrome ramp `#E3E7EF → #9AA3B2 → #E8EDF5` — hero metal treatment
- Cyber lime `#B8FF2E` — signature accent; ~12%
- Hot magenta `#FF3EA5` — ~10%
- Aqua `#3FE0C5` — ~8%
- Sparkle white `#FFFFFF` for stars and glints; ~5%

## Type
- Display: **Michroma** (open license) — wide techno caps, tracking +5%
- Labels and body: **VT323** (open license) pixel bitmap at ≥20px; fallback **Poppins**
- Scale 1.333: 18 / 24 / 32 / 43 / 57
- Display may arc or mirror-flip for the chrome-reflection gag
- Pixel type never below 20px; longer copy moves to the sans

## Layout grammar
- Bubble and pill shapes; rounded everything, blobs welcome
- Centered optimistic hero with symmetric sparkle scatter
- Sticker clusters with hard or chrome shadows, slight rotations ±5°
- Scanline overlay at 2–4% opacity for CRT texture
- Lens flares placed deliberately, 1–2 per composition, as intentional kitsch
- Grids loosen here — rhythm over alignment

## Signature moves
- Liquid chrome display text with a horizon reflection below baseline
- Translucent bubble buttons with inner glow and specular dot
- Pixel-bitmap elements against smooth vectors — the era's signature clash
- Star and sparkle punctuation at type junctions
- Chrome bevel on one hero element max

## Motion & interaction
- 300–500ms bouncy elastic easing on entrances
- Chrome shine sweeps across display text on a 2s loop
- Labels blink or flicker CRT-style at low frequency
- Loops must be seamless; motion reads like a screensaver done well

## Do / Don't
- Do: commit to one hero metal treatment per composition
- Do: balance nostalgia shapes with modern grid sanity
- Do: keep pixel type large and sparse
- Don't: drift into vaporwave purple-and-statue territory — this is chrome and lime optimism, not neon Rome
- Don't: pile gradients until nothing reads as an object
- Don't: set body copy in pixel fonts
- Don't: scatter flares at random; each needs a light source to justify it
- AI failure mode: gradient soup with no hero object plus vaporwave purple — anchor with one chrome treatment, keep lime+magenta+aqua accents, and place flares on purpose

## Prompt handles
- Y2K aesthetic
- chrome liquid metal text
- lens flare kitsch
- cyber lime accent
- bubble UI shapes
- millennium optimism
- pixel and vector mix
- sparkle stars, CRT scanlines

## Field QA (v0.2.0 gallery run)

- Chrome headline on the ice ground is 2.18:1 — midnight navy is the default ground for any chrome-carrying hero; ice is for chrome-free compositions.
- VT323 carries labels ≥24px only; body copy always moves to the sans (now explicit).
- Three families (Michroma + VT323 + sans) is a sanctioned exception to the two-typeface floor — the pixel voice is structural here.

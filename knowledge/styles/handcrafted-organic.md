# Handcrafted Organic

**Identity:** Paper grain, wobbly hand-inked edges, and an earthy palette pulled from the subject itself — the warmth of the handmade at screen scale.
**Temperature:** quiet
**Best for:** artisan food and drink, craft goods, sustainability brands, community projects, warm editorial sites.
**Avoid when:** precision is the message — technical, medical, or luxury-minimal contexts.

## Palette
- Cream paper `#F2EDE3` — ground; ~55%
- Ink `#33291F` — hand-drawn lines, text; ~15%
- Sage `#7C8B5E` — mid tone; ~12%
- Subject-derived accents, ~15%: ochre `#C9982F`, indigo dye `#31406B`, beet `#7C3B4E`, clay `#B4633F`
- Rule: derive accents from the SUBJECT — herbs pick greens, seaside picks slate blues, bakery picks wheat+beet — never default to cream+terracotta on autopilot

## Type
- Display: **Young Serif** (open license) 400 — built-in wonk; Fraunces 500–600 acceptable when chosen deliberately (reflex-overused as display)
- Body: **Work Sans** (open license) 400–500
- Scale 1.25: 16 / 20 / 25 / 31 / 39
- Hand-lettered wordmark or script accent allowed — ONE instance per composition
- Body leading 1.6; keep ≥4.5:1 contrast despite texture

## Layout grammar
- Irregular edges: torn-paper borders, wobble frames drawn with visible pen
- Hand-drawn rules and wavy underlines replace straight hairlines
- Paper grain overlay at 4–8% opacity across the ground
- Asymmetric organic placement; margins may wobble ±8px
- Dashed or stitched borders for grouping
- Imperfection is deliberate — but never at the cost of legibility
- Texture layers: one behind content (grain), one above (stamps, ink marks) — no more

## Signature moves
- Ink illustrations with visible stroke-weight variation (2–5px)
- Rubber-stamp labels rotated 2–3° with imperfect ink coverage
- Torn-paper section edges revealing a second layer
- Hand-drawn arrows and circled annotations
- Grain texture that survives at thumbnail size

## Motion & interaction
- 300–500ms gentle; nothing mechanical or linear
- Stamp-in entrance: scale 1.06 → 1 with a 60ms settle
- Paper-fold transitions between sections
- Hover: ink line redraws itself over 200ms, left to right

## Do / Don't
- Do: sample the palette from the actual subject matter
- Do: keep one hand-lettered moment per composition
- Do: let edges wobble while type baselines stay true
- Don't: default to cream+terracotta every time — the AI cliché this style drowns in
- Don't: over-texture until type muddies; grain stays under 8%
- Don't: fake perfection with clean vectors and uniform strokes
- Don't: mix a third polished sans into the pair
- AI failure mode: the cream-and-terracotta reflex regardless of subject — name the subject, pull two accents from it, and vary the earth tones accordingly

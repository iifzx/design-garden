# Bauhaus

**Identity:** Circle, triangle, square in primary colors doing structural work — geometry as the message, not decoration on top of it.
**Temperature:** bold
**Best for:** cultural events, education brands, startup identities, wayfinding posters that must read across a street.
**Avoid when:** subject is delicate, luxurious, or copy-heavy — geometry crowds nuance and long text.

## Palette
- Paper `#F2EFE8` — warm off-white ground; ~55%
- Cadmium red `#D2372C` — lead primary; ~15%
- Chrome yellow `#F2B807` — ~10%
- Cobalt blue `#1D53C4` — ~10%
- Ink `#191919` — type, rules, outlines; ~10%
- Flat fills only: primaries never blend into gradients, edges stay razor-crisp

## Type
- One geometric sans: Futura ideal; open license: **Jost** (closest match) or **Poppins** 600–700
- Single family total; weights 400–700
- Scale 1.414 (√2) for poster drama: 16 / 23 / 32 / 45 / 64
- Headlines uppercase with +5–10% tracking; body never tracked
- One keyword may rotate 45°; everything else sits square on the grid

## Layout grammar
- Strict hidden grid, then ONE diagonal (45°) axis cutting it for dynamism
- Compose from 3–5 large geometric masses; no confetti-scale shapes
- Let shapes bleed off-canvas — cropping implies a bigger world
- Calculated asymmetry: heavy mass one side, thin diagonal the other
- Overlap flat fills to generate secondary tones at intersections
- Negative space carries the same visual weight as the shapes

## Signature moves
- The triad: circle, triangle, square coexisting at clashing scales
- 45° bars and diagonals implying motion, labor, industry
- Type scaled until it becomes geometry, not text
- Hard overlaps creating depth with zero shadows
- One thin rule or dot acting as the composition's fulcrum

## Motion & interaction
- 200–400ms with straight-line easing cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0.4, 1)
- Hover: shape rotates 45° or scales 1.05 — geometric, never elastic
- Entrances slide along orthogonal or 45° paths
- No blur, no bounce, no dissolve — shapes behave as solids

## Do / Don't
- Do: assign every shape a job — balance, direction, or emphasis
- Do: keep fills flat and edges crisp at every size
- Do: cap the palette at 3 primaries + ink + paper
- Do: scale shapes so at least one spans ≥30% of the canvas
- Don't: add gradients, bevels, textures, or 3D rendering
- Don't: use two different rotation angles in one composition
- Don't: shrink shapes into wallpaper patterns — they must stay structural
- AI failure mode: drifting into Memphis confetti (shapes too small and numerous) or softening with shadows — if you count more than 5 shapes, delete some

## Prompt handles
- Bauhaus geometric poster
- primary colors, circle triangle square
- diagonal composition 45 degrees
- flat shape overlap
- 1920s modernism, form follows function
- geometric sans uppercase
- constructivist balance
- flat color blocks, no gradients

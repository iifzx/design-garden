# Luxury Serif

**Identity:** Didone serif at monumental size, wide-tracked caps labels, jewel accents on deep neutrals, and emptiness that costs money.
**Temperature:** quiet
**Best for:** fashion, beauty, hospitality, jewelry, premium packaging, high-end service brands.
**Avoid when:** product is utilitarian, playful, or mass-market friendly.

## Palette
- Ivory `#F5F0E6` — ground; ~70%
- Charcoal `#1E1A15` — type and rules; ~22%
- Deep emerald `#153F38` — jewel accent; ~5%
- Gold foil `#B99656` — metallic detail; ≤3%
- Warm gray `#9B948A` — metadata only
- Swap emerald for oxblood `#5E1F22` or sapphire `#1F3A5F` per brand

## Type
- Display didone: **Bodoni Moda** (open license); alternative **Playfair Display**
- Body: quiet sans **Inter** 300–400; alternative **Jost**
- Display 64–120px; body 15–16px with leading 1.6–1.7
- Caps labels at +0.2em tracking: "M A I S O N" energy
- No bold in body; hierarchy via size and spacing

## Layout grammar
- Margins ≥25% of canvas width on posters; ≥120px on web
- Single column or firm left axis; one idea per view
- One restrained image: lone object, macro texture, or deep-shadow still life
- Numerals as design details — "No. 04", oversized ordinals
- Centered or asymmetric-left; never busy, never three-column
- A letterspaced numeral or ordinal may stand alone as an entire view on posters

## Signature moves
- Hairline-to-fat stroke contrast of the didone at 96px+
- Letterspaced caps eyebrow above every title
- One jewel or metallic accent placed on a single element
- Product photographed on an empty field with vast margin
- Thin full-width rule under the masthead wordmark

## Motion & interaction
- 400–800ms slow fades; 12px rise on scroll reveals
- Hover: tracking widens +0.05em or color shifts to gold
- Nothing springs, nothing bounces; motion whispers
- Image reveals via slow opacity, never slide

## Do / Don't
- Do: let whitespace signal price before the product does
- Do: keep metallic flat foil or a single subtle sheen
- Do: pair one didone with one quiet sans — nothing else
- Don't: gradient-glow the gold; it cheapens instantly
- Don't: let script fonts sneak in for "elegance"
- Don't: add flourishes, ornaments, or extra rules; flourish budget is zero
- Don't: fill the emptiness with badges, ribbons, or social proof chips
- AI failure mode: glowing gradient gold and calligraphy creep — strip to flat metallic, delete scripts, and double the whitespace you think it needs

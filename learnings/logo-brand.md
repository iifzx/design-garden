## Entries

### 2026-11-20 | Geometric lettermark P read as "D"
- Situation: a bold P built from stem + full circle bowl hid the join at heavy weights — the closed right side won and the mark read D
- Move: shrink the bowl ring (r15/stroke 12 on a 100 grid, counter r9) so bare stem shows below the loop — a P is read by the descender, not by the bowl
- Pitfall: DEAL evidence files drift from the final mark — back-port geometry fixes into the dealt direction file too, so the kept evidence teaches the shape that shipped
- Confidence: repeated

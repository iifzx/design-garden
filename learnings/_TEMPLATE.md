### 2026-11-16 | (example entry — pattern reference)
- Situation: SaaS landing hero with the client's saturated brand purple on a near-black canvas
- Move: raised base surface lightness to L≈0.45 tinted toward the brand hue; reserved brand purple for accents only; perceived contrast jumped and the brand survived
- Pitfall: pure #000 backgrounds kill saturated brand colors — dark canvases need tinted mid-lightness surfaces, not black
- Confidence: user-confirmed
